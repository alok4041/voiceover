THE COURSE GUIDE

Ropebound 3D

A co-op obstacle race inside SquadVoice. Work with your team, stay connected by the rope, and get every runner across the finish.

Your objective: Help your whole team reach the finish before the rival team. The first runner alone does not win the race—your team must make it together.
2–5players on each team
10players maximum per lobby
3team rescues per race

How to start a race

Open Ropebound from your SquadVoice room, then set up the lobby with your squad.

1

Join a team

Players are balanced between Team A and Team B. Each side needs at least two connected runners; a team can hold up to five.

2

Balance the lobby

The host can swap players. The host may allow free team switching; teams cannot be changed after the race begins.

3

Ready up

Every runner must tap Ready up. If a team changes, affected players need to ready up again.

4

Host starts

When both teams have at least two connected players and everyone is ready, the host can tap Start race.

Controls

Use the on-screen controls while the race is in landscape mode.

✣

Four-way movement stick

Push up to run forward, down to slow or move back, and left/right to steer across the course. Ease the stick back to center to stop steering.

↑

Jump

Tap Jump to leap while grounded. Time it for pits, boulders, river gaps, and lava. A jump already in the air cannot be triggered again.

◇

Brace

Press and hold Brace to resist the wind tunnel. Keep moving with your team; bracing slows your forward run.

⚙

Camera and HUD settings

Use Follow, 3D Orbit, or Top camera. Drag the scene to orbit when available. In Settings, adjust HUD transparency, choose Customize HUD to move the stick and action buttons, or reset their positions.

Tip: coordinate over voice chat. Call out the next hazard, wait for teammates who fall behind, and keep an eye on the rope-tension meter.

Course hazards

Hazard order and placement can change between races. Follow the on-screen cue and help your team through each section.

Jump

Spike pit

Build forward speed, then jump over the spikes. Falling into the pit sends your team back to its last checkpoint.

Jump

Path boulder

Jump over the stone. Hitting it while too low bumps you back, so reset your approach and try again.

Keep moving

Rope bridge

Run across the swaying bridge. Do not stop on it: after a short pause the bridge can give way and trigger a team rescue.

Jump

Open river

Run and jump across the gap, aiming to land beyond the water. Falling in regroups the team at its checkpoint.

Hold Brace

Wind tunnel

Hold Brace against the gusts. The wind slows runners who push through without bracing.

Jump

Lava flow

Leap over the lava and keep moving. Landing in it triggers a team rescue.

Race rules

  • Stay with your squad. Teammates are linked by a rope. If runners spread too far apart, the tether pulls them back together; crossing hazards as a group is safer.
  • Checkpoints follow the team. Your team secures progress when its trailing runner reaches a checkpoint. If someone falls, the squad regroups at the latest checkpoint.
  • Rescues are shared. Each team gets three rescues for the race. A fall into a major hazard or a bridge collapse spends one. If a team runs out of rescues and fails again, the other team wins.
  • Run in rhythm for a Squad Surge. On a clear stretch, when everyone is grounded, moving forward, and close together, your squad can charge a brief speed boost. Hazards and bracing pause the charge.
  • The whole team must finish. The first team whose runners all reach the finish wins. If both teams complete together, the result is a tie.
  • Late arrivals wait for the next round. Team changes are locked after the start. Reconnect or join the lobby before the next race to play with a team.

Fair play and voice chat

  • Play as a team: do not intentionally sabotage your squad or exploit bugs to disrupt another team.
  • Keep voice chat respectful. Use the microphone control to mute when needed, and avoid sharing personal or sensitive information in a room.
  • Share room codes only with people you want in your room. Leave the game or voice room when you are done.
Ropebound is a casual team race. Controls, course layout, and timing may be tuned as the game improves; the in-game prompts take precedence if they differ from this guide.
